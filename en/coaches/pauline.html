About Me

"Many stuck points are not due to lack of ability, but because expectations and inner conflicts haven't been sorted out."

I believe everyone holds their own answers. A coach's role is to accompany you through deep listening and powerful questioning to see the current situation clearly, clarify stuck points, and move towards the change you truly desire.

Coaching Focus

  • Self-Awareness & Inner Belief Exploration
  • Self-Labeling & Limiting Beliefs
  • Workplace Challenges, Decision-making Difficulties & Inner Conflicts

My Coaching Style

  • Gentle but Direct: I will appropriately challenge limiting narratives.
  • Safety with Logic: My safety isn't just emotional soothing, but ensuring your logic is caught and your inner self respected.

In coaching conversations, I care about one thing: Helping you translate vague feelings into clear, understandable language.

Professional Background

  • HR & Organizational Development
  • Organizational Transformation, Cross-departmental Communication & Strategy
  • Planning & Executing Internal Workshops and Training
  • Managing tension and transition challenges between individual contributors, professionals, and management roles
  • Lumina Spark Certified Practitioner
