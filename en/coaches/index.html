The ICF credential path

The ICF sets rigorous standards for the global coaching profession. Advancing from ACC to PCC involves not just accumulated hours, but a continuous refinement of the 'Coaching Mindset', 'Maintains Presence', and 'Deep Listening'.

ACC in training

ACC (Associate Certified Coach): Entry level, requiring 60+ training hours and 100+ experience hours. All three coaches are working towards it now.

PCC Goal

PCC (Professional Certified Coach): Professional level, requiring 125+ training hours and 500+ experience hours (the goal after ACC).

Coaching Mindset: The coach is a partner, not an expert. We believe clients have the potential for self-growth and problem-solving.
