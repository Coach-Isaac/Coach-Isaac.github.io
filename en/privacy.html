Privacy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

We care about your privacy. This page explains what information we receive when you use this site and book a session, how we use it, and how you can see or delete it.

Site analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages help. It uses no cookies and does not track individuals; we only see anonymous totals such as page views, country and device type.

Outside services

Fonts come from Google Fonts and the analytics script from Cloudflare. When a page loads, your browser connects to their servers.

Settings kept in your browser

Your language and light or dark mode choice are stored only in your own browser and never sent to us.

Booking form

Booking uses Google Forms, so your answers are stored by Google. Isaac, one of our coaches, receives the form and passes only what is needed to arrange the session to the coach you chose. We use this information only to arrange and contact you about sessions, and do not share it with any other third party.

Session content

What you share in sessions is kept confidential. For the coach's ICF credential review, sessions are recorded, and may be transcribed, only with your consent; these are used only for that review. You can decline recording and still have your session. Under the ICF Code of Ethics, confidentiality does not cover illegal activity, disclosure required by law, or imminent danger to you or others.

Emailing a coach directly

If you email a coach directly, only that coach receives it; it is stored by the coach's email provider (such as Gmail) and used only to answer you.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your information only as long as it is needed. You can email us at any time to see, correct or delete it.
